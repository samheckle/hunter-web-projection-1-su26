## Project 3 Proposal Feedback

### Part 1

As a class, we will write out questions and workshop how to ask for feedback. 

Take 10 minutes to write down 3-5 questions that would be helpful for others to answer. To construct effective questions, think about:

- How to clarify the focus of your project
- What have you been asking yourself when constructing the proposal?
- What parts of your project do you think you need more brainpower to answer the question?
- What specific impressions are you trying to give your audience?
- Do you need suggestions on a particular area of a project?
- What are you worried the most about the project?
- Avoid: Asking questions that have a clear yes/no answer
- Avoid: "am I a good artist?" which can fall under many forms such as "is the project compelling?", "is the piece good?", "is the project working?". Instead interrogate what makes a project "good" to *you* and ask whether or not your project is achieving those goals.

Some good example questions:
- What resonates to you in the narrative?
- If you had to give this project a title, what title would you suggest?
- Where do you see the connections and disconnections in this group of photos?

## Part 2

As a class, let's workshop the questions. 

Everyone will put their questions in the chat and we will go over the effectiveness of them.

## Part 3

In your breakout rooms, talk about your projects and the questions we built together as a class. 

Write your feedback and your answers to their questions as a reply to your discussion post. Include any links to reference projects you find relevant.
